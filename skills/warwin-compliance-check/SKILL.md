# Warwin compliance check

Rules are summarized from `/workspace/warwin/research/skills-research.md` section 3 (sources checked 2 Nov 2026) and the brain pages `compliance/content-bans.md` and `compliance/platform-policies.md` (which carry the latest decisions). If the last check is over 90 days old, re-verify Res. SIC 446/2025 + 271/2026, CABA Ley 6.330 / LOTBA, Buenos Aires IPLyC and the platform policies before relying on this. Also re-verify when national bills move. This is a design check, not legal advice; flag open legal questions to Edgar. `warwin-marketer` also runs a compliance check on every piece; agree with it, and the stricter verdict wins.

## Checks (any miss is a FAIL with a fix)
1. **Channel:**
   - organic, paid or affiliate
   - LinkedIn is organic only, with no offers or deposit CTA
   - Meta paid needs ad-account gambling authorization and 18+ targeting
   - X paid needs certification
   - TikTok is sports betting only
2. **Legend:**
   - Paid and affiliate pieces need "EL JUGAR COMPULSIVAMENTE ES PERJUDICIAL PARA LA SALUD" + "+18": full width at the bottom, at least 10% of the height, bold, high contrast, inside the safe zone, and on screen for the whole duration of any video or Story.
   - CABA-targeted pieces also need "EL JUEGO COMPULSIVO ES PERJUDICIAL PARA VOS Y TU FAMILIA" plus the LOTBA helpline.
   - Organic pieces need at least "+18 | Jugá con responsabilidad". Organic pieces with a bonus or a sexy wardrobe use the full solid legal band (about 203px on a 1080x1350 post: 135px main line + 68px second line "+18 | Aplican TyC | Jugá con responsabilidad"), and the band is opaque so it covers any bikini bottom.
3. **Offer:**
   - only "Hasta 350% en tus primeros 3 depósitos", with "Hasta" visible
   - "Aplican TyC"
   - paid offers also need "PARA MÁS INFORMACIÓN O LIMITACIONES APLICABLES CONSULTE EN warwin.casino" (at least 5% height)
   - no old 100%, no ARS amounts, no free spins
4. **Content bans:**
   - minors or youthful cues
   - linking gambling to success, money or a luxury lifestyle, or false expectations or odds
   - alcohol or tobacco (including drinks in a girl's hand)
   - athletes, celebrities, real clubs, crests, stars, the AFA or sportswear brand marks (Adidas etc.), player names or numbers like 10
   - invented wins, testimonials or certificates
   - cash, payment logos
   - outdoor placement for CABA
5. **Fictional character (CABA art. 31.f):** the brand girl in paid ads targeting CABA is FLAGGED until counsel clears it. She's allowed in organic posts and in responsible-gaming messaging.
6. **Sexual content (Meta ad standard):**
   - **Paid Meta:** FAIL on lingerie or swimwear, see-through clothing, cleavage as the focus, body-part crops, suggestive poses (bending, lying, lip-biting), wet looks, bedroom settings, or innuendo in copy. The only boost-safe wardrobe is W09.
   - **Organic, site and approved affiliates** (Edgar wants sexy looks: W11b, W12, W19, W21 family, W22): allowed only with waist-up or three-quarter framing, no bending, lying or lip-biting poses, no body-part crops, no wet look, cleavage not the focus, open jacket or trench is fine, bikini bottoms under the solid legal band or out of frame, and never boosted. Corset or very low-cut looks are the highest risk.
   - Mark every sexy piece "organic only" in its file and in the brain.
7. **Brand and character rules:**
   - logo untouched with clear space, real SVG, not generated text
   - crown has 5 peaks (composite the v8 crown)
   - skin looks real, not smoothed
   - Argentine tee has wide vertical stripes with no crest or brand
   - one main object, Spanish accents and ¿¡ correct, voseo, game names not translated
   - nothing important cropped, safe zones respected, correct size and file weight
8. **Output** a pass/fail table (check, result, fix) and an overall verdict: Ready, Ready for organic only, or Blocked.
